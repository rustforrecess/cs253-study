Parts of a Machine Instruction

The instruction addu $10, $9, $8 is stored as one 32-bit pattern. Each field of that pattern carries one of the four parts: the operation, the first source, the second source, and the destination.

operation (opcode) source 1 ($9) source 2 ($8) destination ($10)
stage 0 / 4
Press Play or Step to see which bits carry each part.
